How to Amend MRB (Multi-Room Bookings)

Overview

This SOP explains how to perform amendments on Multi-Room Bookings (MRBs) in DOTWConnect, depending on the client type and supplier type.

MRBs made by XML clients or WebBeds Admin users are handled per room, like individual bookings. Both Amend Booking and Amend Offline are available.
  • Date Amendments apply to all rooms in the booking. Cannot change dates for one room only.
  • Only the first room allows date adjustments.
  • Other rooms have date fields deactivated.
  • All other amendments can be done on a per room basis without affecting other rooms.
  • Name Amendments: Per room basis without affecting other rooms.
  • All other amendments: Can only be changed on a per booking basis, affecting all rooms.
  • Nightly booking cost, selling price, and compensation show totals for all rooms.
