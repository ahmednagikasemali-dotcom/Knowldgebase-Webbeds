Refund Handling & Tools

Step-by-step guide for supplier refunds and calculation tools.

Calculation Rule

To process a refund correctly, always follow this formula:

Refund = (Original Price) - (Cancellation Fees)

Quick Steps

1
Download the Expedia Calculator from the toolbar above for complex cases.
2
Update the system with the new net amount and add comments.
3
Notify the accounts department if a manual VCC refund is needed.
