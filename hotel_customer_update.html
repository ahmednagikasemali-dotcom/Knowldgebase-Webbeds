Hotel / Customer Info Update – Steps

📝

Start: Update Booking / Customer Info

🔍

Step 1: Verify Booking Details

Check booking ID, customer name, dates, room type, and hotel info.
📌 Example: Customer calls to update arrival date → verify original booking details first.
📞

Step 2: Confirm Customer Request

Ask for exact change: name, dates, room preferences, or contact info.
📌 Example: Customer wants to change check-in date → confirm new date with them.
🏨

Step 3: Update Hotel System / GDS

Update booking according to customer request in the hotel system.
📌 Example: Update arrival date, special requests, room type in PMS/GDS.
✉️

Step 4: Notify Customer

Send confirmation email / SMS about updated booking details.
📌 Example: Email customer: "Your check-in date has been updated to …"
🗒️

Step 5: Add Notes / Internal Record

Add clear notes on ticket / CRM for audit and future reference.
📌 Example: "Customer requested early check-in, updated in PMS on 16-Jan-2026."
