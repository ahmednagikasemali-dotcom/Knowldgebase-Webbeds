How to Amend Booking Online

Overview

This SOP explains how to perform an online booking amendment using the Amend Booking functionality in DOTWConnect.

This applies to bookings from Direct Hotel Contracts or DMC / Local Suppliers. Dynamic, Third Party, or Channel Manager bookings must be amended offline.
  • Booking source must be Direct Contract or DMC/Local Supplier.
  • Booking must be pre-travel.
  • Amendment deadline must not have passed.
  • Open the booking in DOTWConnect.
  • Scroll to the bottom of the page.
  • Click Amend Booking.
  • If unavailable, check Amend Offline.
  • Review booking summary displayed at the top.
  • Note the Room Cost / Selling Price.
  • Name correction
  • Date amendment
  • Occupancy amendment
  • Meal plan amendment (subject to contract availability)
  • Special requests via system may reprice the booking.
Send special requests directly to the hotel or supplier to avoid price recalculation.
  • Click Proceed with the Amendment.
  • Review rate plans, cancellation deadlines, and prices.
  • Only offer rate plans with Room Available status.
  • Confirm with hotel if status is Room on Request.
  • Advise customer of additional selling price.
  • Confirm amendment penalty and new cancellation deadline.
  • Proceed once customer agrees.
  • VCC and invoices update automatically.
  • Monthly invoicing updates supplier & client invoices.
