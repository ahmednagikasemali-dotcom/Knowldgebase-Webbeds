Virtual Payments from WebBeds

Complete Operational Guide & Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Our Commitment

WebBeds has made a commitment to pay suppliers with electronic virtual card numbers (VCCs) from WEX, Citi and HSBC—this will reduce manual payment processes and ensure suppliers are paid in a faster and more secure manner.

Introducing VCCs from WEX, Citi and HSBC

  • Single-use electronic card numbers with transactional data attached.
  • Secure and controlled payment method.
  • Globally accepted by suppliers via existing point-of-sale (POS) terminals.

Benefits to the Business

  • Easy to use with no development required: Process VCCs via your existing POS system as a card-not-present transaction.
  • Saves time and frees up account’s resources: A unique card number per transaction and the ability to customise data recorded can help you automate reconciliation work.
  • Payments received faster: Automated payment processes facilitate faster receipt of payment, which can help improve cash flow.
  • Risk of fraud reduced: VCCs are single-use, and controls can be used to avoid fraudulent use.
  • Guarantees payment: As VCCs facilitate faster payment, you do not need to worry about late or non-payments.
  • Reduces the cost of receiving payment from overseas: You will receive payment via a VCC in your local currency, which avoids fees for accepting payment in a different currency.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to know if VCC was charged or declined by using DOTWConnect? How to know the reason of decline? If a card is charged successfully or not, it will be shown in the transaction logs & real time authorisations & decline table.
Transaction Type: D = Charged | C= Refund.
How to have DOTWConnect Access? This should be provided upon onboarding, if not available then request via Service Desk.
What can you see in the DOTWConnect as a Customer Service Agent? Everything relevant to the booking and card history, status, transaction logs is available to CS team. Only difference is the card details, CVC and expiration date are masked.
Is there a time limit on when a VCC can be charged? Yes, the validity of our cards for Flexi-rate bookings is usually from check-in date up to check-out + 30 days. For Non-Refundable bookings, the validity is from booking creation up to check-out + 30 days.

Operational Procedures

How to request a VCC to be deleted?

vCarly users should be able to have the DELETE Access in DOTWConnect. Steps to close:

  1. In finance tab, Choose Virtual CreditCards then select Close VCC.
  2. Enter the booking you want to close, and the reason then click Close Card.
  3. Wait for the confirmation that card has been closed successfully.
