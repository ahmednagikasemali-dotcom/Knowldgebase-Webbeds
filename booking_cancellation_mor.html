SOP – MOR Client Cancellation Requests

❓

Main Tab: Answering Queries

Sub Tab: Cancellation

Description:
Step-by-step guide for handling cancellations and waivers in DOTWConnect.

Purpose

This guide explains the steps to handle MOR client cancellation requests, system rules, verbiages, and QA process.

  • Travel Pass Group: Cancel booking on client’s behalf, inform of applicable charges, check for waiver, update client with final resolution.
  • Reservations.com & Deal Peak: Ask client to accept policy or cancel themselves. Once client replies with "PLEASE CANCEL THIS RESERVATION AND PROCEED WITH ESCALATION", cancel, assist waiver, update client.
Client must send clear verbiage to proceed.
  • Travel Pass Group: Ask client to accept penalty or cancel themselves. Cancel only if explicitly requested.
  • Reservations.com & Deal Peak: Ask client to accept penalty or cancel themselves to assist with waiver.
  • Waiver Partial Refund: Process per supplier authorization.
  • Waiver Full Refund: Process per supplier authorization.
  • Cancelled before deadline: "Booking cancelled free of charge, preauthorization reversed, no charges applied."
  • Cancelled after deadline, full refund: "Booking cancelled free of charge, refund processed shortly. Follow up: DOTWrefunds@webbeds.com"
  • Cancelled with partial refund: "Booking cancelled with XXX night charge, refund for remaining nights processed shortly. Follow up: DOTWrefunds@webbeds.com"
  • Always send supplier authorization/proof to refunds team.
  • No offline cancellations allowed; only proceed via system.
  • Waiver requests only start if:
    • Booking canceled from our side
    • Booking post-stay
    • Booking has cancellation error
  • Any phone communication must also be confirmed via email.
  • If refund obtained and client informed by phone, reply via email and send to refunds team.
  • Same applies to HCN and other updates.
  • All final resolutions communicated by phone must also be shared by email.
  • Step 1: Receive Cancellation Request
    • Receive cancellation request via email, booking platform, or customer support channel.
    • Record booking reference and client details.
  • Step 2: Verify Booking Details
    • Confirm booking reference.
    • Confirm client/company name.
    • Confirm hotel name and stay dates.
    • Ensure requester is an authorized contact for the client.
  • Step 3: Check Booking Status
    • Identify current booking status: Confirmed, On request, or Cancelled.
    • Check whether booking is refundable, non-refundable, or partially refundable.
  • Step 4: Review Cancellation Policy
    • Review hotel cancellation policy and WebBeds contractual terms.
    • Identify cancellation deadline and applicable fees.
  • Step 5: Communicate Terms to Client
    • Inform client whether cancellation is allowed.
    • Inform client of any cancellation charges.
    • Inform client of refund amount if applicable.
    • Obtain client confirmation to proceed.
  • Step 6: Process Cancellation in System
    • Cancel booking in WebBeds system.
    • Notify supplier if required.
    • Update booking status to Cancelled.
  • Step 7: Refund Handling (If Applicable)
    • Process refund based on original payment method and agreed commercial terms.
    • Advise client of expected refund timeline.
  • Step 8: Send Cancellation Confirmation
    • Send written cancellation confirmation to the client.
    • Include booking reference, cancellation date, fees charged (if any), and refund details.
  • Step 9: Record & Documentation
    • Log cancellation reason.
    • Save all client and supplier communication.
    • Ensure documentation is available for audit and reporting purposes.
