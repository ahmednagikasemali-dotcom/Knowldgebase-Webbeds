SOP – Booking Statuses (DOTWConnect)

📄
❓
Total Booking Statuses: 6
System: DOTWConnect

Overview

There are 6 possible booking statuses that a DOTWConnect booking can have, depending on the travel date or how the availability was taken. The booking status is always visible in the short booking summary.

Applies to any pre-travel booking taken from confirmed availability from suppliers.

  • WebBeds can request assistance from the hotel/supplier
  • WebBeds can request the hotel confirmation number
If a confirmed booking is rejected by the hotel, WebBeds has the right to request assistance or relocate the guest to another hotel.
If a booking is successfully amended with confirmed availability, the status becomes AMENDED CONFIRMED.

Applies to any pre-travel booking taken:

  • Above confirmed availability or allocation
  • When hotel/room type is on stop sale

Hotels have the right to decline ON REQUEST bookings.

Hotels may accept the booking:
  • On the same rate
  • On a higher room type
  • On a different rate
If the hotel cannot accept the booking, the customer must create a new booking.

Applies to any ON REQUEST booking that has not been accepted by the hotel/supplier.

  • Rejected on same rate
  • Rejected on higher room type
  • Rejected on different rate
A WebBeds user can manually change the status from ON REQUEST to DENIED using the Enter Reference functionality.

Applies to any booking cancelled before the travel date.

  • Cancelled Before Customer Deadline (No charges)
  • Cancelled After Customer Deadline (With charges)
The cancellation type will always be indicated in the booking status.

Applies when a customer attempted to book but the booking was not completed.

  • Usually caused by technical issues
Equivalent to Failed Booking status in other WebBeds platforms.

An old booking status that applied to bookings:

  • Past check-in date/time
  • Not cancelled prior to travel

Hotels/suppliers had the right to request payment for such bookings.

Due to financial system updates, this status is no longer used for bookings created from 2023 onwards.
