WebRev Failures Handling

Operational guide for identifying and managing WebRev process failures.

Instructions

1. Email is sent to operations team with failure description.
2. Identify what kind of failure occurred.
3. Follow below guide for handling failure.

Failure Types & Actions

Type of Failure Required Action
Creation of new booking with supplier fails Original booking needs to be checked if all ok and make sure no other booking was created (neither shell or any similar).
Cancellation of new booking in case of price validation failure Original booking number needs to be checked and to cancel the new booking.
Cancellation of original booking failure To cancel the new booking and leave the original untouched (as at this stage bookings are identical but have 2 different bkg refs).
Linking of the bookings failure To check with customer and supplier if they have the reservation correctly according to the original booking then to cancel the new booking.
Fatal Error To check with customer and supplier if they have the reservation correctly according to the original booking and in case new booking exists then to cancel the new booking.
