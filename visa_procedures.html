Process: Prepayment

  1. Client sends email to visa-support@webbeds.com
  2. Check documents
  3. create a MISC with the requested Visa type and ask the client to pay and waiting untill he sent te payment confirmation
  4. Upload VISA details on DOTW
  5. Add booking ref to email subject
  6. Send email to supplier (VISA TYPE/NATIONALITY/BOOKING REF)
  7. Agent processes payment & provides proof within 12h
  8. Send Visa request to Miracle Tourism LLC
  9. Send VISA document back to client after receiving from supplier
