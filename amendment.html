Amendment Decision Tree

Commentary & Tips

  • Amendments include any modification: extras, supplements, refunds, compensation, etc.
  • 10% mark-up applies on any added service (min 5 EUR/USD or equivalent).
  • Handling depends on: Cancellation Policy, Booking Type, Cash Direction, System Feasibility.
  • Offline services must be "Accepted & Confirmed" to avoid auto-cancellation.
  • Always link added services to the original booking.
  • Pre-payment clients must settle payment before using VCC to settle provider.
  • If currency differs, convert first, then apply mark-up.
  • Always confirm MISC services after client payment/settlement.

Amendment Flow

Allowed – Steps

  1. Check if amendment is free or chargeable.
  2. If required, send amendment request to supplier and wait for confirmation.
  3. Process amendment in the system (add mark-up, update prices & data).
  4. Inform client and ensure MISC/Offline service is confirmed or Generic is "Confirm without taking from allotment".
  5. For pre-payment clients, ensure payment is settled before VCC use.
  6. Link the service to the original booking.
  7. Update client with confirmation and HCN if applicable.

Not Allowed – Steps

  1. Check policy (Non-refundable / restricted amendment).
  2. Inform client amendment cannot be processed.
  3. Offer alternatives if available.

Regarding MOR Clients:

  • No amendments or changes with a higher price.
  • For any amendment with a lower rate / refund / partial refund, confirm by mail then send to refund team to raise refund and adjust system.
  • Actions are performed on DOTW similar to GSA & VIP booking.

DOTW Amendment Flow

  1. Online Amend: add service using booking engine under same ITN.
  2. Offline Amend: request supplier quotation, apply mark-up, offer to client.
  3. Client approves → create offline booking if cash goes to new entity.
  4. Ensure service is "Accepted & Confirmed" to avoid auto-cancellation.
  5. Update system (data + price) and client.

SH Amendment Flow

  1. Mostly offline handling required.
  2. Request quotation from supplier, apply mark-up, offer to client.
  3. Client approves → confirm service, update system and client.
