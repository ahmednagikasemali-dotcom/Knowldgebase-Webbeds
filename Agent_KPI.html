UCC Evaluation – Starting January 2026

1. Ethics & Integrity (5/5)

  • Loss of $1 or above → 0/2
  • No Loss → 2/2
  • Case that might lead to a loss → 0/2
  • No cases → 2/2
  • Less than 7.5 hrs on F9 OR less than 6.5 hrs on FD → 0/1
  • 7.5 hrs or more on F9 AND 6.5 hrs or more on FD → 1/1

2. Communication (5/5)

  • 85% or above → 2/2
  • 75% to less than 85% → 1/2
  • Less than 75% OR 2 Auto-Failed Calls → 0/2
  • 85% or above → 1/1
  • Less than 85% → 0/1
  • Meets communication standards → 2/2

3. Adaptability

  • More than 1 emergency → 0/1
  • Flexibility with shifts → 1/1
  • More than 10 incidents pending for over 10 days → 0/2
  • Within acceptable limits → 2/2
  • SLA achieved (90%) for 72 hours → 1/1
