Agent Evaluation Checklist – WebBeds

  • Did you greet the customer correctly within the first 10 seconds?
    📝 Script: "Thank you for calling WebBeds. My name is [xxx]. How can I help/assist you?"
  • For transfer calls, did you acknowledge holding correctly?
    📝 Script: "Thank you for holding" or "Thank you for waiting" AND introduce yourself: "My name is [xxx]" or "You are through to [xxx]"
  • Did you verify booking details early in the call (first 20%)?
    📝 Script: "To ensure I have the correct booking, can you please verify hotel/property name, guest name, travel date?"
    OR "Can I verify the booking details with you? May I have hotel name, guest name, arrival date?"
  • Did you follow the On-Hold process correctly?
    📝 Steps: Ask client if okay to be on hold, thank after hold.
    Script: "Can I put/place you on hold?" or "Please hold" AND "Thank you for holding" or "Thank you for your patience"
  • Did you perform a proper Warm Transfer with correct info and expectations?
    📝 Steps: Inform client transfer is needed, share verified booking, concern, collected info.
    Script Examples:
    "Hi Kristine, I’m going to transfer you to our Inresort team, where Darwin will assist you. Please hold."
    "Thank you for your patience, April. I’ll transfer you to Kathleen, who can resolve your issue. Please stay on the line."
  • Did you show empathy, assurance, and proper acknowledgement?
    📝 Example Statements:
    Empathy: "I am sorry you are having this problem."
    "I realise how upsetting this must be."
    Assurance: "I'll be delighted to help you."
    "Don’t worry, you have the best person on the phone."
    Acknowledgement: "I want to make sure I fully understand your request…"
  • Did you recap the customer's concern before closing?
    📝 Script: "To summarize, you want to modify the date from March 03-05, 2025 to May 16-18, 2025. Is this correct?"
  • Did you investigate all available resources?
    📝 Steps: Contact hotel/supplier/customer, check SM/CM/IT, review tickets, booking vouchers, hotel's website, WB platform, follow Freshdesk guidelines.
  • Did you document all findings clearly?
    📝 Steps: Add complete notes in system/Freshdesk, update notes and tags, enter correct entries in Loss Tracker.
  • Did you transfer the case to the correct folder/agent?
    📝 Step: Identify customer location and send to correct agent/folder.
  • Did you provide accurate and clear information to hotel/customer?
    📝 Step: Share necessary information according to issue need.
  • Did you endorse the case properly for the next agent?
    📝 Step: Use notes and tags in Freshdesk to guide next agent.
  • Did you exhaust all means to provide a resolution?
    📝 Step: Collaborate with internal/external stakeholders. Avoid pushing back on client.
  • Did you process refund or compensation correctly?
    📝 Step: Adjust rate correctly, inform supplier/customer about amount.
  • Did you follow cancellation/amendment process correctly?
    📝 Step: Follow full guidelines in User Guide manual.
  • Did you categorize and disposition the case/call correctly?
    📝 Step: Ensure accurate categorization and call disposition.
  • Did you provide complete and accurate resolution?
    📝 Step: Tailor resolution to customer concern.
  • Did you send emails/updates to the correct recipient?
    📝 Step: Check email addresses before sending.
  • Did you use correct terminology for suppliers?
    📝 Step: Use "hotel provider", "hotel partner", "partner" instead of generic terms.
  • Did you maintain professional communication?
    📝 Step: Avoid rudeness, sarcasm, profanity; maintain appropriate tone.
