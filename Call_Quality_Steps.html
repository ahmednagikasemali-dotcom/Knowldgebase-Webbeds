Call Quality – Inbound Calls (WebBeds)

  • Confirm the caller reached WebBeds.
  • Professional greeting and name introduction.
  • Friendly and confident tone.
📝 Script:
“Thank you for calling WebBeds. My name is (Agent Name). How may I help you?”
  • Travel agency name confirmed.
  • Guest names verified.
  • Hotel name and travel dates confirmed.
⚠️ Do not confirm booking details on behalf of the client.
  • Client request clearly identified.
  • Clarifying questions asked if needed.
  • Client approval obtained before action.
  • Permission requested before hold.
  • Reason and duration explained.
  • Client updated regularly.
📝 Script:
“May I place you on hold for 2–3 minutes while I check this for you?”
⚠️ Never place a client on hold without permission.
  • Client informed before transfer.
  • All relevant details shared.
  • Seamless handover ensured.
  • Caller information reviewed.
  • No repetition of questions.
  • Immediate assistance provided.
  • All actions completed.
  • Nothing missed.
  • Positive final impression delivered.
📝 Script:
“Is there anything else I can help you with?
Thank you for calling WebBeds. Have a good day!”
  • No raised voice or sarcasm.
  • No negative language.
  • Always show empathy.
✅ Use Instead:
“I understand how frustrating this must be.”
“Let me check that for you.”

These words and phrases must be avoided when speaking with customers. They are grouped by severity level:

Severity Description Examples / Phrases
RED 🔴 Completely unacceptable. Do not use under any circumstances. “Shut up”, “Annoying”, “I don’t care”, “Calm down”, “Check yourself”,
“Don’t shout at me”, “I will hang up”, “Absurd”, “What is it this time”,
“Stop calling”, “Stop chasing”, “Listen to me”, “You listen”,
Any equivalent phrase in all languages
AMBER 🟠 Strongly discouraged. Sounds rude, defensive, or dismissive. “I don’t want to”, “I have said already”, “I told you before”, “I told you many times”,
“I will not do that”, “I won’t do that”, “Not my duty”, “Not my job”,
“Not my responsibility”, “Not my problem”, “Let me tell you”, “You are wrong”,
“You should have checked”, “You should have done”, “What do you want”
UNDESIRED ⚫ Not forbidden, but creates a negative customer experience. “Cannot do”, “Can’t do”, “Can’t help”, “Cannot help”, “Cannot assist”,
“I can’t assist”, “[XXX] is not here”, “I do not know”, “I don’t know”,
“Do it on our website”, “Not my case”, “Not possible”, “Impossible”,
“You need to send”, “Our policy”, “Our company policy”,
“Not something I can help”, “Not something I can do”, “Not my area”, “I repeat”
