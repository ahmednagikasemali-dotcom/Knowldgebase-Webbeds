Amend a Miscellaneous Booking

Overview

This SOP explains how to amend an existing Miscellaneous booking using the Miscellaneous functionality in DOTWConnect.

  • Go to the Operations tab.
  • Click on the MISC tab.
  • Select REQUESTS from the drop-down list.
  • The Look Up Miscellaneous Details page will load.
  • Locate the DOTW MISC # field.
  • Enter the required MISC number.
  • Click APPLY FILTER.
Searching by DOTW MISC # is the fastest way to retrieve a booking.
  • A summary of the booking will appear.
  • Click the VIEW button.
  • The full Miscellaneous booking details will load.
  • Click the EDIT MISC button.
  • Amend booking details, travel dates, cancellation deadline, and cancellation penalty percentage.
  • Click the CHANGE PRICE button.
  • Amend supplier cost, markup percentage, and selling price.
  • Click UPDATE to save changes.
Ensure pricing amendments are approved before updating.
