DOTWConnect Manual V012025

Comprehensive guide for Sales, Contracting, and Customer Service teams.

Homepage Overview: The middle part of the site displays Bookings Info, Products Info, and Customers Info reports. Access level determines visibility for Sales and Management.

MAIN MENU DESCRIPTIONS

Located at the top of the page, providing access to core functionalities:

USERS: Search and view profiles of MyAdmin users and their granted accesses.
FINANCE: Conversion rate logs, buffers, and transaction reports.
PRODUCTS: View lists of suppliers for Accommodation, Transfers, and Tours.
CUSTOMERS: Search client profiles and account management.
MARKUP: Access customer/network markups for specific territories.
OPERATIONS: Search, view, and action existing bookings; run reports.
REPORTS: Pull reports on Cancellation Deadlines and Forward bookings.

FINANCE: E-COMMERCE TRANSACTIONS

Used to track failed bookings due to credit card issues (B2B2C clients).

How to pull the report:

Click Finance Tab > Ecommerce Transactions Link.

Common Filters:

  • Customer Name: Search by specific client.
  • Amount Between: Filter by the specific amount range.
  • Card Status: Filter for authorization failures or issues.
  • Filter Dates By: Select Payment Dates (Recommended for card failures).
  • Payment Date Range: Start from 1 day before and 1 day after the mentioned charge date.
Output: Choice of "VIEW REPORT" (on-page) or "VIEW IN EXCEL". Look at STATUS and TRANSACTION ID for error reasons.

PRODUCTS: ACCOMMODATION SUPPLIERS

Access supplier profiles, contracts, and payment information.

Filtering Profiles:

Search via Country, City, Chain, Brand, Accommodation Status (Live/Non-Live), or Unit Name.

Status Categories:

ACTIVE Open for bookings.
NON-ACTIVE Currently disabled.
TEMP CLOSED Temporarily unavailable.

Booking Channels (Supplier Types):

  • OFFLINE (DOTW): Direct Contract (DC) or Local Suppliers/DMC.
  • DIRECT: Dynamic / Hotel Chain connections.
  • THIRD PARTY: Intercompany or external 3rd party providers.
  • CHANNEL MANAGERS: Direct connections through Channel Manager software.

PROPERTY & SETUP INFORMATION

Property Set-up (Middle Page):

  • Account Manager: WebBeds Manager handling the property.
  • Property Type: Defines if it's a Hotel or Serviced Apartment.
  • Default Currency: Currency used for payments to the hotel.
  • Star Rating: Official rating with short description.

Contact Details:

Available for Direct Contracts (DC). Includes Main Reservation Email (for LPOs), Finance contact, and Hotel Website.

Payments Section:

  • Market Policy: Rate basis (Guest Nationality, Residence, or Client Location).
  • Use VCC: Shows if the hotel accepts Virtual Credit Cards.
  • Loading Time: When the VCC is loaded with the full amount.

TRANSFER AND TOUR SUPPLIERS

Filter via Country, City, Status, or Supplier Name.

Service Search:

Search by Supplier Profiles or Services Offered. Live suppliers appear first in results.

VIEW Button: Summary of the service without entering booking module.
RATES Button: Check live rates for specific dates.

Payments Section:

Use VCC: Yes (Accepts VCC) | Use VCC: No (Bank Transfer required).

CUSTOMERS TAB & SETUP

Search via Company Name, Country, City, or Account Manager.

Account Management Sections:

  • Default Currency: Client's main payment currency.
  • Payment Method: Pre-pay, Credit, or Credit Card.
  • Credit Line: Shows Available Credit and Billing Limit (for Credit clients).

XML & Booking Setup:

  • XML Access: Confirms if client is connected via XML Documentation.
  • Restrictions: Shows restricted rates or minimum night stay requirements.
  • Markups/Discounts: Details additional percentages added for specific hotels or travel periods.

NEW BOOKING CREATION

Two types: New Booking (Live Availability) and Offline/Misc (Manual creation).

Accommodation Search Steps:

  1. Click New Booking > Accommodation Booking.
  2. Select Customer Profile (Use 'Main User' if not specified).
  3. Input Destination (City, Hotel, or Landmark).
  4. Input Dates & Occupancy (Child ages are required).
  5. Set Nationality & Residence (For accurate market-specific rates).
Note: You can search for Multiple Rooms by clicking the Blue Plus button. All rooms must be in the same hotel and for the same dates.
